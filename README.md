An entry for [make.fun](https://make.fun)'s
`u-motasimfuad-the-worst-pizza-topping-062c6893` bounty: one funny line
that pitches a terrible pizza topping, in a single line of at most 200
characters.

## The line

> New: pizza topped with homework. It arrives cold, nobody finishes it, and your mum still makes you eat the whole slice.

The line is 119 characters. The joke works by selling homework the way
a pizzeria sells a new topping, then letting the topping behave exactly
like homework always does: it shows up cold, nobody can finish it, and
someone in authority still insists you eat every last bit. The pitch
voice is part of the gag — it sounds like a real menu announcement for
something nobody would ever order twice.

## The program

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) prints the line and
writes it to `/output/entry.txt`, after asserting it is still one line
of at most 200 characters:

```bash
python3 main.py
```

Output, verified locally:

```text
New: pizza topped with homework. It arrives cold, nobody finishes it, and your mum still makes you eat the whole slice.
```

119 characters, 1 line, 0 homework left uneaten.
